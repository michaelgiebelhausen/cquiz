# Exam runbook

## Before students arrive

Confirm the private instructor login and live provider test. Import the actual approved question bank and roster, preserving leading zeros in student IDs. Review every prompt and weighted insight; allow enough unique questions for all tables and rounds. Set the 50/30/20 default weights or your preferred weights, round/mingle timing, and invitation window. Settings and content freeze when the exam is created.

Try a synthetic rehearsal separately from the actual exam. Check actual student phones with the speech preflight on the classroom HTTPS URL. Verify attendance physically: email and school ID identify an uploaded roster entry but do not independently authenticate institutional identity. Students should not share IDs. Release an old student session from the instructor console when a legitimate device replacement is needed.

## During the exam

Start when present participants are joined and ready. Each question asks for one insight. Students volunteer during a fixed window; response speed gives no benefit. The app selects a volunteer using current standing and prior contribution count. A speaker sees their collected text and presses Finish contribution. Tablemates complete all three competence ratings before their next contribution opportunity. Flagged transcription remains included in evaluation and is marked for instructor review.

Watch connection status, pending/failed grading, table progress, and question exhaustion. Pause to handle interruptions. A local server restart pauses the clock for inspection; Vercel cold starts preserve the clock. Reconnecting students see saved state. If the app auto-submits an unfinished contribution at a round boundary, it preserves the last text accepted by the server. Unsaved words cannot be reconstructed.

At each mingle, students give every tablemate the two private composite presentation ratings, then move to their assigned next table. Presentation ratings remain diagnostic and do not change mastery, ranking or groups. Historical competence ratings are recalculated as the raters' demonstrated standing changes.

## Finish

Allow accepted responses to finish grading. Retry failed jobs after resolving provider issues. Check flags and unresolved competence/transition ratings. Export rankings, retained points, component scores and full evidence. Results remain provisional if assessment evidence or convergence is unresolved. Keep the evidence and database backup private in the project. cquiz does not publish institutional grades.

If a phone cannot transcribe after preflight, resolve its browser/device issue before the student contributes; typed correction and audio-upload fallbacks are intentionally absent. If network or provider service fails, pause the exam and restore service. Do not substitute synthetic rehearsal grading for actual students.
